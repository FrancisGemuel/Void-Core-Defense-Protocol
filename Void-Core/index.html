■ Mech — slow, heavy damage, ground only
■ Drone — widest range, hits air only

About

A tower defense game built with vanilla JS and Canvas API. Place towers, control your hero, and survive 20 waves of increasingly tough enemies — bugs, dinos, blobs, flyers, and roamers.

Barracks towers deploy 5 low-damage soldiers. Soldiers patrol near the barracks, chase and shoot enemies inside the barracks range, then return to their posts. Fallen soldiers respawn after 20 seconds.
