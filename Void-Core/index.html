TOWER DEFENSE defend the base. survive all 20 waves.

How to Play

🖱️Click to move your hero
⇧+ClickShift+Click to place a tower
🎯Click an enemy to attack it directly
QPress Q for hero AOE burst
▶Start button begins each wave
🏰Don't let enemies reach the base!
■ Sniper — long range, single target
■ Flamer — short range, splash damage
■ Mech — slow, heavy damage, ground only
■ Drone — widest range, hits air + ground

About

A tower defense game built with vanilla JS and Canvas API. Place towers, control your hero, and survive 20 waves of increasingly tough enemies — bugs, dinos, blobs, and flyers.
